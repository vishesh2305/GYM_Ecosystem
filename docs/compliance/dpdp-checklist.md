# DPDP Act 2023 checklist

How the platform meets the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) as a data fiduciary. Every item
that software can prove points to the test that proves it; items that are processes, not code, say who owns
them and when. Test paths are relative to `apps/api/test/` unless they start with `packages/`.

| #   | Obligation                                                                                                 | How                                                                                                                               | Proof (passing test)                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Consent is recorded per purpose with the notice version (s.6)                                              | `consents` keeps every decision with policy version, IP and device                                                                | `consents.e2e.test.ts` › records each decision and shows the latest one per purpose                                                                                               |
| 2   | Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, and is kept as history (s.6(4))                               | Same endpoint, `granted: false`; withdrawal adds a record                                                                         | `consents.e2e.test.ts` › keeps the full history: withdrawing adds a record instead of deleting one                                                                                |
| 3   | Health data is processed only with consent; on withdrawal processing stops and the data is erased (s.6(6)) | Sync refuses without `health_data` consent; withdrawal deletes workouts, records, plans and body metrics                          | `training.e2e.test.ts` › health data consent › is needed to save training, and withdrawing it erases the health data                                                              |
| 4   | Right to access: a summary of the personal data held and its processing (s.11)                             | `POST /v1/me/privacy/exports`: every personal-data table from the registry, across all gyms, credentials removed                  | `privacy.e2e.test.ts` › data export › contains every kind of personal data held about me, and nothing about anyone else                                                           |
| 5   | Right to correction (s.12)                                                                                 | People edit their profile (`PATCH /v1/me`); gyms correct member records (`PATCH …/members/{id}`)                                  | `sessions.e2e.test.ts` › returns and updates the signed-in user's profile; `members.e2e.test.ts` › updates only what was sent                                                     |
| 6   | Right to erasure, except where law requires keeping data (s.12)                                            | `POST /v1/me/privacy/erasure`: deletes or anonymises per the registry; keeps tax and payment records tied to an anonymised member | `privacy.e2e.test.ts` › account erasure › removes or anonymises me in every module, keeping only what the law requires                                                            |
| 7   | Erase data once its purpose is served (s.8(7))                                                             | Hourly retention job with `RETENTION_DAYS`                                                                                        | `privacy.e2e.test.ts` › retention › clears personal data that has served its purpose, and nothing that is still needed                                                            |
| 8   | Know where all personal data is (supports 4, 6, 7)                                                         | `packages/db/src/personal-data.ts` registry of every table                                                                        | `packages/db/src/personal-data.test.ts` › classifies every table in the schema exactly once; says why any personal data is kept after erasure; can read every personal-data table |
| 9   | Reasonable security safeguards: one gym can never see another's data (s.8(5))                              | Postgres RLS per gym, per person; checked for every gym endpoint                                                                  | `cross-tenant.e2e.test.ts` (every endpoint); `packages/db/src/tenancy.test.ts`; `packages/db/src/sync-rules.test.ts`                                                              |
| 10  | Safeguards: staff and platform admins use two-step verification; secrets encrypted                         | MFA required for staff with member data and for admins; TOTP secrets AES-GCM encrypted                                            | `staff-mfa.e2e.test.ts`; `admin.e2e.test.ts` › is only for platform admins who used two-step verification; `crypto.test.ts`                                                       |
| 11  | Access by the platform's own staff only with the gym's consent, read only, audited                         | Owner-granted, time-limited support access; every view audited and counted                                                        | `admin.e2e.test.ts` › support access (all three tests)                                                                                                                            |
| 12  | Sessions can be ended by the person; erasure signs them out everywhere                                     | Session revocation, sign out everywhere                                                                                           | `sessions.e2e.test.ts` › sign out everywhere ends every session of the user; `privacy.e2e.test.ts` (401 right after the erasure request)                                          |
| 13  | Personal data breach: notify the Board and affected people (s.8(6))                                        | Process: incident runbook with the Board (72 h report), people and CERT-In (6 h) notices; alerts detect incidents                 | Runbook `docs/runbooks/incident.md`; alerts `infra/terraform/modules/observability` and CloudWatch (ADR 019). Notices: owner founders                                             |
| 14  | Grievance officer and contact published (s.8(10))                                                          | Process: named officer in the privacy policy and app                                                                              | Not code. Before launch (D1); owner: founders                                                                                                                                     |
| 15  | Children (under 18): verifiable parental consent, no tracking (s.9)                                        | **Open.** App accounts do not record age yet; gyms record members' dates of birth                                                 | **Not yet covered.** Needs an age question at sign-up and a parental-consent flow (Stage C onboarding). Until then, the app's terms restrict accounts to 18+                      |
| 16  | Processors (SMS, WhatsApp, email, payments) bound by contract                                              | Process: data processing agreements with MSG91, Meta, AWS, Razorpay, Expo                                                         | Not code. Before launch (D1); owner: founders                                                                                                                                     |
